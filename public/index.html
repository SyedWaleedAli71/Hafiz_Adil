Our Story

A Neighbourhood Favourite in Gulbahar #2

Hafiz Adil Rabri House is a family sweets shop at Plot #1646, Near Khajji Ground, Gulbahar #2, Karachi.

Our counters carry the classics people grew up with — delicate Ras Malai, creamy Shahi Qulfa, hearty Sohan Halwa, refreshing Falooda — alongside everyday treats and savouries. Simple things, done patiently and well.
