About Hafiz Adil Rabri House

A neighbourhood favourite for traditional sweets, creamy ice cream, and rich rabri — right in the heart of Gulbahar #2, Karachi.

Who We Are

A Passion for Flavour, A Commitment to Quality

Hafiz Adil Rabri House is a sweets and ice cream shop located at Plot #1646, Near Khajji Ground, Gulbahar #2, Karachi. We specialise in traditional South Asian sweets, freshly churned ice cream, cooling falooda, rich rabri, and savoury samosa.

Our menu is a celebration of beloved flavours — from delicate Ras Malai and creamy Shahi Qulfa to hearty Sohan Halwa and refreshing Falooda. Every item is prepared with quality ingredients and care for the craft.

We welcome customers looking for everyday treats as well as those seeking sweets for Eid, weddings, and family celebrations. Come visit us, or reach out directly via phone or WhatsApp to place a large order.

Small Details, Big Difference

Great sweets are never rushed. We cook patiently, taste as we go, and hold every batch to the same standard our customers have come to expect.

  • Fresh every day. Prepared in batches so what you buy is what we'd serve our own family.
